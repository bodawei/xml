### 4.3.1 The Text Declaration

External parsed entities may each begin with a text declaration.

> **1.0/2: Replaces:** External parsed entities should each begin with a text declaration.

*Text Declaration*

```
[77]  TextDecl ::= '<?xml' VersionInfo? EncodingDecl S? '?>'
```

The text declaration must be provided literally, not by reference to a parsed entity. No text declaration may appear at any position other than the beginning of an external parsed entity.

> **1.0/2: Appends:** ...entity. The text declaration in an external parsed entity is not considered part of its replacement text.

> **1.0/3 Replaces:** The text declaration must be provided literally, not by reference to a parsed entity. The text declaration must not appear at any position other than the beginning of an external parsed entity. The text declaration in an external parsed entity is not considered part of its replacement text.
